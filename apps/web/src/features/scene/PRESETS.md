# Scene presets: provisional list for ratification

**Status:** the architecture is approved provisionally; these IDs are **not final**. Every preset is written only in values from the shared catalog (`docs/scene/scene-effect-catalog.v1.json`), and `vocabulary.test.ts` enforces that. A preset never contains raw colors. What a value looks like is defined once in `swatches.ts` and shared by all presets and model overrides.

**Rendering profiles**
- **V0 (today):** `PALETTE_ADAPTER_V0`, everything vibeTheme's existing `applyPalette()` can render without map changes (ground, buildings, roads, water, parks, sky colors, light color, global grade, label system). It covers 128 of 828 variations.
- **V1 (proposed):** V0 plus light intensity and angle, sky blend, fog color and transition timing. It needs a `buildMapStyle` hook from the map owner and covers 148 variations.
- **Later phases:** atmosphere overlays, markers, UI tokens, motion and 3D set pieces. A requested setting a profile can't render is **disabled and reported**, never shown as active.

## Visual identities

Each entry gives the intended look, then what renders today (V0) and what is still pending.

### `neutral`
The everyday map and the safe fallback. It chooses nothing, so the weight left over in any blend is the plain map.

### `amber_refuge`: lamplit, bookish warmth
- **Look:** honeyed ground, terracotta roofs, brick-amber walls warming toward the tallest towers, warm-gold road casings, autumn parks, a golden-hour sky with a warm horizon band, candle-warm grade with cream highlights and brown shadows. Dark-warm labels.
- **V0 renders:** all of the colors above (18 settings).
- **Pending:** warm vignette, fine film grain and dust motes (atmosphere renderer); warm pin halo (markers); warm-frost panels and amber accent (UI); drifting particles (motion); light preset and ambient fill (V1+).

### `noir_jazz`: midnight, smoky, intimate
- **Look:** near-black slate ground, slate walls darkening with height, coal roads with ink casings, midnight-navy water, moonlit parks, a night-indigo sky with a faint warm horizon, desaturated high-contrast "filmic" grade with navy shadows and gold highlights. Light labels.
- **V0 renders:** 19 settings. **V1 adds** dim side light.
- **Pending:** wet sheen on streets, whisper fog in blue, cinematic vignette and grain, a noir render treatment, a warm pin halo and dimmed weak matches.
- **Naming note:** "jazz" may suggest music props, but jazz-specific 3D layouts require verified venue context under PR #33's activation rules, and this preset requests none. Consider a mood-only name (e.g. `noir_nocturne`) before finalizing.

### `neon_city`: electric violet, magenta and cyan after rain
- **Look:** deep violet-black ground, glass-violet walls cooling with height, neon-dark roads with hot-pink casings, purple-twilight water, a purple sky with a magenta neon horizon band, vibrant dramatic grade with violet bias. Light labels.
- **V0 renders:** 19 settings. **V1 adds** back-lit pink light.
- **Pending:** magenta building outlines, strong major roads, rainy-night wet sheen, cyan rim light, neon sparkle and subtle scanlines, neon pin halo and a top-match pulse, dark-glass neon-edged panels.
- **3D set piece:** `cyberpunk_night` (vibe-selected, verified model IDs). It needs a 3D renderer and is hidden while navigating.

### `dream_pastel`: rose and lilac soft focus
- **Look:** blush ground, pastel walls and roofs, ivory roads, aqua water, storybook parks, a dream-pastel sky with a rose band and diffuse horizon, soft low-contrast grade with rose bias and peach highlights.
- **V0 renders:** 17 settings. **V1 adds** bright pink light and the soft horizon blend.
- **Pending:** dreamy bloom, pearl whisper fog, drifting petals, magical sparkle, soft pin halo, rose accents.
- **3D set piece:** `fantasy_market` (shared with `woodland_story`).

### `sunlit_coast`: high-key daylight on the harbor
- **Look:** sun-washed ground, white plaster walls and cream roofs, ivory roads, turquoise water, lush summer parks, golden sand, a clear blue sky with a pale mist horizon, a gently warm, slightly vivid grade. Cool-dark labels.
- **V0 renders:** 15 settings. **V1 adds** high-key overhead light.
- **Pending:** waterfront haze, subtle lens glare, glass panels, teal accent.

### `woodland_story`: calm greens and morning light
- **Look:** earthy ground, sandstone walls and copper roofs (muted low-rise), sandstone roads, storybook parks and deep-forest woods, deep-teal water, a warm-dawn sky, matte low-contrast grade with teal shadows.
- **V0 renders:** 18 settings. **V1 adds** low morning light.
- **Pending:** illustrated tree canopies, storybook park glow, sunlit dust and fireflies, paper panels and forest accent.
- **3D set piece:** `fantasy_market`.

### `underground_indie`: concrete, sodium light, xeroxed grit
- **Look:** pale monochrome ground with coal roads, concrete walls darkening with height, slate roofs, silver water, winter-muted parks, a storm-gray sky with a faint warm band, desaturated dramatic grade with warm-gray shadows.
- **V0 renders:** 18 settings. **V1 adds** dim amber side light.
- **Pending:** coarse film grain, strong vignette, halftone print, cross-processed finish, outlined pins, ink-dark panels.

### `retro_atlas`: a well-thumbed paper map
- **Look:** parchment ground, sandstone walls and terracotta roofs, ivory roads with inked casings, sage parks and olive woods, pale aqua water, a muted flat-print grade with cream highlights and brown shadows. Warm-dark labels.
- **V0 renders:** 18 settings.
- **Pending (most of its identity):** aged-parchment paper texture, editorial-atlas render treatment, retro sky gradient, paper grain, editorial label font. **Ratification note:** until the atmosphere renderer and font provisioning ship, this preset reads as a warm, muted palette rather than a paper map.

### `minimal_modern`: cool glass, nothing extra
- **Look:** cool-blue ground, concrete walls with cool towers, slate roofs, ivory roads, silver water, sage parks, a clear sky with a cool horizon band and a crisp horizon, a muted cool grade with ice-blue highlights. Cool-dark labels.
- **V0 renders:** 18 settings. **V1 adds** bright overhead light and the sharp horizon.
- **Pending:** only glass panels and the cyan accent. **Ratification note:** it is deliberately restrained, and the closest preset to `neutral`.

## Questions before finalizing IDs
1. Keep music- and cuisine-flavored names (`noir_jazz`) or move to mood-only names, given PR #33's context-only activation rules for music props?
2. Are `retro_atlas` (texture-dependent) and `minimal_modern` (near-neutral) distinct enough for the first release, or should either be swapped for a preset that renders strongly in V0?
3. Should seasonal or observance moods ever become presets? The current answer is no: those layouts are explicit-request only.
